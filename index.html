Curriculum Expectations (Ontario Grade 2)

This app directly supports Structures and Mechanisms: Simple Machines and Movement expectations:

  • B1. Assess impact of simple machines on daily life and the environment.
  • B2.1 Describe ways in which giant/simple machines make work easier.
  • B2.2 Identify six simple machines (lever, wheel/axle, pulley, inclined plane, wedge, screw).
  • B2.3 Identify mechanisms that change the direction or amount of force applied.
  • B2.4 Demonstrate how forces (pushes and pulls) cause movement or change speed/direction.
